# Consultant Mailbox

**Purpose.** Manual bridge for Consultant messages, research handoffs, and
responses from the project owner or Validator.

**Ground rules.**

- Append-only. Do not edit another role's entries.
- Sign every entry with role and date.
- Keep newest entries at the bottom.
- New entries must use the collapsible mailbox envelope defined in
  `agent-relay/roles/consultant/protocols/CONSULTANT_MAILBOX_PROTOCOL.md`.
- Consultant work is exploratory unless promoted by the project owner or
  Validator.
