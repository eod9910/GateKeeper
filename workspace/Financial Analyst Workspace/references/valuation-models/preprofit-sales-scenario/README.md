# Pre-Profit Sales Scenario Valuation

Use this folder for references that teach Ledger how to value high-growth companies with negative or unstable current free cash flow.

## Primary Anchors

- revenue growth
- gross margin
- unit economics
- path to contribution margin
- path to operating margin
- cash runway
- financing need
- dilution risk
- EV/Sales and scenario-based terminal economics

## Use When

The company has meaningful revenue or strategic optionality, but current free cash flow is not normalizable.

## Do Not Use

Do not present a fragile DCF as high confidence when cash flows are currently negative because the company is still scaling.
