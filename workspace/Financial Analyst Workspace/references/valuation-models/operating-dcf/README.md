# Operating-Company DCF

Use this folder for references that teach Ledger how to value normal operating companies with meaningful, normalizable cash flows.

## Primary Anchors

- revenue
- operating margin
- operating cash flow
- free cash flow
- reinvestment intensity
- discount rate
- terminal growth
- bear/base/bull scenario range

## Use When

The company has a conventional operating model and cash flows are economically meaningful.

## Do Not Use When

The company is a bank, insurer, REIT, commodity reserve business, special situation, or pre-profit growth company where current free cash flow is not the right valuation anchor.
