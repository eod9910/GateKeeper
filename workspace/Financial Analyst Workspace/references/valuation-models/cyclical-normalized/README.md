# Cyclical Normalized Earnings Valuation

Use this folder for references that teach Ledger how to value cyclical businesses through a cycle rather than off one peak or trough year.

## Primary Anchors

- mid-cycle revenue
- normalized margins
- normalized EPS or EBITDA
- replacement-cycle demand
- utilization
- operating leverage
- balance sheet survival
- normalized P/E or EV/EBITDA

## Use When

The company operates in a cyclical industry such as autos, semiconductors, chemicals, steel, shipping, machinery, homebuilding, or other cycle-sensitive sectors.

## Do Not Use

Do not annualize peak margins or trough losses as if they represent steady-state economics.
