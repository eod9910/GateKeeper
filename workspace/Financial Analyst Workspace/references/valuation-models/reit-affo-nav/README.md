# REIT AFFO / FFO / NAV Valuation

Use this folder for references that teach Ledger how to value REITs and REIT-like real estate operating structures.

## Primary Anchors

- FFO per share
- AFFO per share
- dividend coverage
- net asset value
- cap rates
- occupancy
- same-store rent growth
- lease quality and tenant concentration
- fixed-charge coverage
- debt maturity ladder and refinancing risk

## Use When

The company is a REIT or derives value primarily from income-producing real estate.

## Do Not Use

Do not treat low current ratio, low cash balance, or negative free cash flow from property acquisition as ordinary operating distress. Standard FCF DCF is usually not the primary model.
