# Commodity / Resource NAV Valuation

Use this folder for references that teach Ledger how to value reserve-based or commodity-price-driven businesses.

## Primary Anchors

- reserves or resources
- production volumes
- commodity price deck
- lifting costs or cash costs
- sustaining capex
- depletion
- asset retirement obligations
- NAV by asset or mine/field

## Use When

The company is primarily driven by oil, gas, mining, royalties, reserves, or commodity extraction economics.

## Do Not Use

Do not value a reserve-based business only from trailing earnings without checking commodity price assumptions and reserve life.
