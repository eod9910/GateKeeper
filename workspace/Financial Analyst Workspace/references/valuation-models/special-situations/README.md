# Special Situations / Deal-Value Analysis

Use this folder for references that teach Ledger how to evaluate event-driven situations.

## Primary Anchors

- deal price
- spread to deal price
- probability of close
- timing
- break value
- regulatory risk
- financing risk
- shareholder vote risk
- CVR terms
- restructuring or liquidation value

## Use When

The company is governed by a merger, acquisition, go-private transaction, bankruptcy, restructuring, spin-off, liquidation, CVR, or another hard event.

## Do Not Use

Do not treat a signed deal or restructuring as an ordinary standalone equity setup. Standalone valuation can be useful, but the event terms come first.
