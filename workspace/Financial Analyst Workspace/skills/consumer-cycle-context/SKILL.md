# Consumer Cycle Context

## Purpose

Read the company through the repo's consumer-cycle taxonomy so Ledger can explain whether the business belongs in a highly cyclical, mildly cyclical, or stable bucket and why that matters in a slowdown.

## Rules

1. Use the repo consumer-cycle buckets as the source of truth:
   - `highly_cyclical`
   - `mildly_cyclical`
   - `stable`
2. State the specific category when available, such as:
   - `motor_vehicles_parts`
   - `furnishings_household_equipment`
   - `recreational_goods_vehicles`
   - `transportation_services`
   - `business_equipment_investment`
   - `residential_investment`
   - and the other repo-defined categories
3. Explain what the bucket means for slowdown behavior:
   - `highly_cyclical` = demand usually weakens early and macro deterioration matters more
   - `mildly_cyclical` = demand can soften, but usually later and less violently
   - `stable` = demand is usually more resilient through recessions
4. Treat consumer-cycle context as macro-positioning evidence, not as a replacement for filing-backed business analysis.
5. If the symbol profile and the macro monitor conflict, say so explicitly:
   - example: the company is cyclical, but its category is currently still green
   - or the company is defensive, but the broader monitor is deteriorating elsewhere
6. Keep the conclusion actionable:
   - whether the name should usually be preferred in slowdown
   - avoided in slowdown
   - or treated as mixed / timing-sensitive
