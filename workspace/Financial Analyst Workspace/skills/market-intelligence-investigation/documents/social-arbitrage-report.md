# Social Arbitrage Report

## Purpose

Use this document when a Social Arbitrage or narrative-cluster card is investigated.

Social Arbitrage is about micro perturbations: niche community enthusiasm, product adoption, real user behavior, specialist chatter, migration from topic-indexed spaces into ticker-indexed spaces, and undercovered company implications.

## Required Sections

1. **SOCIAL SIGNAL**
   - Explain what community behavior changed, where it changed, and how abnormal it is versus baseline.

2. **EVIDENCE QUALITY**
   - Evaluate source breadth, authenticity, alias risk, cross-platform confirmation, and whether chatter appears organic or promotional.

3. **COMPANY / THEME MAPPING**
   - Map the claim to tradable tickers and distinguish direct beneficiaries, indirect beneficiaries, and incumbents at risk.

4. **LEDGER CROSS-CHECK**
   - Use valuation, business quality, earnings, balance sheet, options, and filings to decide whether the social signal maps to a playable setup.

5. **BOTTOM LINE**
   - Classify as monitor, research, entry watch, actionable, or reject.
   - Name invalidation.
