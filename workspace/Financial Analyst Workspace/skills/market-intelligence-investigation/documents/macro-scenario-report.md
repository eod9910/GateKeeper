# Macro Scenario Report

## Purpose

Use this document when a Macro Engine scenario is investigated.

Macro scenarios are big-world perturbations: weather, drought, crop failure, commodity shock, energy disruption, strikes, ports and shipping chokepoints, war, sanctions, policy, rates, liquidity, inflation, credit, insurance, and catastrophe events.

## Required Sections

1. **MACRO SIGNAL**
   - Explain the disruption, geography, timing, affected commodity/sector/channel, and why it matters now.

2. **EVIDENCE QUALITY**
   - Prioritize primary data, official agencies, reputable news, specialist sources, and source breadth.

3. **EXPOSURE MAP**
   - Identify first-order winners/losers, second-order beneficiaries, substitutes, suppliers, customers, and hedges.

4. **LEDGER CROSS-CHECK**
   - For mapped tickers, use valuation, financial quality, options flow, earnings sensitivity, and balance sheet risk.

5. **BOTTOM LINE**
   - State whether there is a playable expression now or whether the thesis is real but not yet tradable.
   - Name invalidation.
