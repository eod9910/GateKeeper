# Trade Forensics

## Purpose

Audit completed trades for execution quality and behavioral drift.

## Framework

For each review, separate:

- Planned
- Actual
- Deviation
- Diagnosis

## Evaluate

1. entry compliance
2. stop compliance
3. execution rule compliance
4. exit quality
5. sizing compliance
6. planned R versus realized R
7. behavioral override flags

## Never do

- judge a trade purely by P&L
- excuse rule breaks because the trade made money
- use emotional praise or blame
