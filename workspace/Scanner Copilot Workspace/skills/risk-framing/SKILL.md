# Risk Framing

## Purpose

Translate the current setup into trigger, invalidation, and target logic.

## Rules

1. Respect the hierarchy of structure:
   - local pattern completion,
   - neckline or trigger break,
   - major structure failure.
2. A neckline break by itself can produce only a short countertrend push. Do not treat that alone as proof that the broader trend ended.
3. If dominant structure is still intact, say the pattern may have already finished serving as a pullback and the key question is whether trend resumption is underway.
4. If levels are missing or unclear, state what price action would confirm or invalidate the idea instead of inventing precision.
5. Prefer concrete risk language over vague optimism.
