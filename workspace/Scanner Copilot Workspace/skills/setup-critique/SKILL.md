# Setup Critique

## Purpose

Judge setup quality like a trader, not like a cheerleader.

## Rules

1. Separate what looks structurally valid, what still needs confirmation, and what weakens the idea.
2. Do not force a bullish interpretation just because the scanner candidate came from a bullish detector.
3. Pattern presence alone is not regime change.
4. For reversal-looking shapes, ask in order:
   - did the pattern actually complete,
   - did it get real follow-through,
   - did major structure truly fail.
5. Inside a dominant uptrend, head-and-shoulders-like structures are often better treated as pullback markers than as durable short signals.
6. If price is reclaiming back toward the right-shoulder zone after a shallow neckline break, treat that as possible trend resumption, not automatic bearish confirmation.
7. Only upgrade a bearish pattern to regime-change risk when there is actual structural failure, not just a visible bearish shape.
