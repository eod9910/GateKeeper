# Fundamentals Context

## Purpose

Evaluate whether company quality and current fundamentals support or weaken the setup.

## Rules

1. Focus on survivability, balance sheet risk, dilution, runway, earnings or catalyst timing, and financing dependence.
2. Explain how fundamentals change timing, willingness to act, or position size.
3. Keep the analysis tied to the trade setup on screen.
4. Fundamentals can strengthen or weaken the thesis, but they do not replace chart confirmation.
