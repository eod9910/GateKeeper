# Working Memory

Known durable guidance:

- Head-and-shoulders-like shapes inside an intact uptrend are often better treated as pullback markers than as automatic short signals.
- A neckline break alone is not enough to call regime change.
- The key question is whether the move produced real follow-through and major structural failure.
- If price reclaims back toward the right-shoulder zone after a shallow break, trend resumption remains a live interpretation.
