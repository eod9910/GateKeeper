The important question: what kind of pullback?
----------------------------------------------

This is where the system lives or dies.

There are at least four versions.

### 1\. Trend pullback

You only buy discounted stocks that are already in an uptrend and are retracing.

For example:

*   50-day MA above 200-day MA
*   price above 200-day MA
*   current price between 5% and 15% below 52-week high
*   current pullback into 20-day or 50-day zone

This is the cleanest version if you want less junk.

The tradeoff is that truly deep-value names often will not qualify.

### 2\. Base pullback

You look for discounted stocks that have already stopped collapsing and are now building a base.

For example:

*   discounted fundamentally
*   6-month range compression
*   volatility contraction
*   no new lows for X bars
*   maybe higher low relative to prior panic low
*   breakout or pullback inside the base

This is better for catching early re-accumulation.

### 3\. Mean-reversion pullback inside quality names

You restrict to fundamentally strong companies and buy temporary weakness.

For example:

*   high ROIC
*   positive earnings quality
*   moderate debt
*   price pulls back 8–15% from local high
*   RSI weakened but not structurally broken
*   long-term trend intact

This is probably the most robust version for large-cap names.

### 4\. Deep-value reversal

You look for severely discounted stocks that are in a late-stage decline but showing reversal traits.

For example:

*   huge valuation discount
*   washed-out sentiment
*   selling climax / volume spike
*   first higher low
*   reclaim of key moving average or structure level

This has the biggest upside and the most garbage.
